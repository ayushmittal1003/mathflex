## Class 11 Maths for JEE — intro
Class 11 maths is the foundation of your JEE preparation. Chapters like Functions, Trigonometry, Sequences & Series and Conic Sections build the tools every Class 12 topic depends on. Get these right, and calculus, coordinate geometry and algebra in JEE Main and Advanced become far easier. Learn each chapter step by step, with video lessons, practice questions and notes.

## Class 11 study approach
- Start with Sets, Relations & Functions and Basic Maths/Logarithms; everything else builds on them.
- Do Trigonometry early and thoroughly; it appears across the whole syllabus.
- Finish each part, then practise its questions the same day.
- Revise with chapter-wise PYQs once a chapter is complete.
- Keep a formula sheet per chapter and revise it weekly.

## Class 12 Maths for JEE — intro
Class 12 maths carries some of the most scoring topics in JEE: Calculus, Vectors & 3D Geometry, Matrices and Probability. These chapters reward clear concepts and lots of practice. Master them chapter by chapter to score well in both your board exams and JEE Main & Advanced.

## Class 12 study approach
- Begin with Limits, Continuity & Differentiability; the rest of calculus depends on it.
- Practise Integration daily; speed comes from volume.
- Pair Vectors with 3D Geometry, and Matrices with Determinants.
- Balance boards and JEE: learn concepts once, then add JEE-level problems.
- Solve full chapter PYQs before moving on.

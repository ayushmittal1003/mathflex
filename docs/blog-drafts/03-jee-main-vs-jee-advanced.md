**JEE Main is the first exam. It is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) and leads to NITs, IIITs and other institutes, and it is also the qualifier for JEE Advanced. JEE Advanced is the second exam, held for admission to the IITs.** If you want an IIT, you need to do well in JEE Main first, and then qualify for and write JEE Advanced.

*Everything about JEE Main below comes from the official NTA Information Bulletin for JEE Main 2027. Everything about JEE Advanced comes from the official JEE Advanced 2026 brochure and website, because the 2027 details were not out yet when this was written.*

## What is JEE Main?

JEE Main has two papers. **Paper 1** is for admission to undergraduate engineering programmes (B.E./B.Tech) at NITs, IIITs, other centrally funded technical institutions and institutions funded or recognised by participating state governments. **Paper 2** is for B.Arch and B.Planning. NTA has been responsible for JEE Main since 2019.

Most students mean Paper 1 when they say "JEE Main", so that is what we cover here.

## What is JEE Advanced?

JEE Advanced is the exam for admission to the IITs. Every year one IIT acts as the organising institute. In 2026 it was IIT Roorkee, and the exam was held on Sunday, 17 May 2026. Admission to the IITs follows JEE Advanced and the JoSAA counselling process.

## How JEE Main leads to JEE Advanced

The link is simple: JEE Main Paper 1 is the qualifier. To be eligible for JEE Advanced, you must be among the **top 2,50,000 successful candidates (all categories) in the B.E./B.Tech paper of JEE Main**. This applies to JEE Main 2027 in the NTA bulletin. So everyone who writes JEE Advanced has already written JEE Main.

## Exam pattern and marking

### JEE Main (Paper 1)

JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 is a computer-based test of 3 hours, with Maths, Physics and Chemistry given equal weightage:

- **75 questions, 300 marks** in total.
- Each subject has **Section A** (20 multiple-choice questions) and **Section B** (5 numerical-value questions), so **25 questions and 100 marks per subject**.
- **+4** for a correct answer, **−1** for a wrong answer, and **0** if you leave it unanswered or marked for review. This is the same in Section A and Section B.
- In Section B you type an integer answer, rounded to the nearest integer.

In the maths section, your score is:

$$\text{Maths score} = 4 \times (\text{correct}) - 1 \times (\text{wrong})$$

For example, if you got 18 right and 4 wrong out of 25, your maths score would be $4 \times 18 - 4 = 68$ out of 100. (This is just an example to show the arithmetic.)

### JEE Advanced

JEE Advanced 2026 had **two papers, Paper 1 and Paper 2, of three hours each, and writing both is compulsory**. Each paper has Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics sections. The brochure says the questions are designed to test comprehension, reasoning and analytical ability, that **negative marks may be awarded for some questions**, and that the exact marking scheme is shown in the instructions on exam day. So, unlike JEE Main, you should not assume a fixed +4/−1 pattern for Advanced. Always read the instructions at the start of the paper.

## Eligibility and attempts

**JEE Main 2027:** there is no age limit. You can appear if you passed Class XII (or equivalent) in 2025 or 2026, or if you are appearing in 2027. Candidates who passed Class XII in 2024 or earlier are not eligible. JEE Main is held in two sessions (January and April 2027), you do not have to write both, and if you write both, your best NTA score is used.

**JEE Advanced:** besides being in the top 2,50,000 in JEE Main, the 2026 rules say a candidate can attempt JEE Advanced **a maximum of two times in two consecutive years**. There were also rules about age and the year you first appear in Class XII. The 2027 rules had not been announced when this was written, so please check [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in/) for the latest.

## Key dates for JEE Main 2027

NTA has published these in the Information Bulletin:

- **Session 1:** exams on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027.
- **Session 2:** exams on 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 April 2027.

Check the NTA website for any changes before you plan around them.

## How the maths differs

The maths syllabus overlaps a lot: algebra, calculus, coordinate geometry, vectors, 3D geometry and probability appear in both. The difference is in what the exam asks of you. JEE Main has a fixed structure with 25 maths questions, one mark pattern and a numerical section. JEE Advanced, in the brochure's words, tests comprehension, reasoning and analytical ability, and its marking can vary. So Main rewards accuracy and speed across many questions, and Advanced rewards a deeper grip on each idea. ## FAQs

### Do I have to write JEE Main to write JEE Advanced?

Yes. You must be among the top 2,50,000 successful candidates in the B.E./B.Tech paper of JEE Main to be eligible for JEE Advanced.

### Can I get into an IIT through JEE Main alone?

No. The IITs admit through JEE Advanced. JEE Main Paper 1 leads to NITs, IIITs, other centrally funded technical institutions and state-funded or recognised institutions.

### Are there negative marks in JEE Main maths?

Yes. You get −1 for a wrong answer in both the multiple-choice and numerical sections, and 0 for unanswered questions.

### Can I write both sessions of JEE Main?

Yes, you can. You do not have to, and if you write both, your best NTA score is used.

### Who organises JEE Advanced?

One of the IITs acts as the organising institute each year. In 2026 it was IIT Roorkee. Check jeeadv.ac.in for the latest.

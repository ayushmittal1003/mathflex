**MathFlex is a JEE maths platform for Class 11 and 12 students. Every chapter is taught in short video parts, followed by practice questions, and you pay only for the chapters you actually need.** Every chapter also has a free 3–4 minute preview, so you can see how it is taught before you spend anything.

## Who MathFlex is for

MathFlex is built for students in Class 11 and Class 12 who are preparing for JEE Main and JEE Advanced, and also for droppers who want to fix specific chapters. It works well for parents too: you can see exactly what your child is buying, one chapter at a time.

Most students are not stuck on the whole syllabus. They are stuck on three or four chapters. MathFlex is made for that situation.

## How it works, chapter by chapter

Each chapter is split into short parts instead of one three-hour lecture. Here is what a part looks like:

1. **Watch.** Watch the part at your own pace. Only the time you actually watch counts, so skipping ahead does not unlock anything.
2. **Practise.** Once you have watched most of the part, its practice set opens: practice problems and past JEE questions on exactly what you just learnt, with worked solutions.
3. **Move on.** Clear the practice and the next part opens.
4. **Revise.** Notes and formula sheets come with every chapter you own, and you can rewatch any part as often as you like.

You can browse every chapter, for both classes, on the [chapters page](/chapters).

## Buy only what you need

This is the main idea behind MathFlex. You can buy:

- **Single chapters**, each with 3 months of access. Good when you are stuck on one topic, say Complex Numbers or Probability.
- **Full courses:** Class 11 Complete or Class 12 Complete, each with 1 year of access, or the Class 11 + 12 combo, with 2 years of access.

If you need only a few chapters, buy them one by one. If you need most of a class, a full course is usually the better deal. The [pricing page](/pricing) shows the current prices and what each option includes, and the [courses page](/courses) lists the full courses.

## Leaderboard and streaks

Studying alone gets boring, so MathFlex adds a little game on top. You earn XP for watching parts and solving questions, build a daily streak, collect badges and see where you stand on the weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboard. It is optional, but many students find that a streak is a good reason to open the app on a lazy day.

## A 1:1 call with Karan

If you want help with your study plan or have doubts you cannot clear alone, you can book a 45-minute one-to-one call with Karan. Bring your doubts, your chapter list and your plan, and work out the next steps together. You can book it on the [book a call page](/book-a-call).

## How is this different from a big coaching batch?

Big coaching batches are built around a fixed timetable and the whole syllabus. MathFlex is built around you:

- **Your pace.** There are no fixed batch timings. Watch when you are free, and rewatch what you did not understand.
- **Your chapters.** You do not pay for chapters you already know well.
- **Practice built in.** Every part ends with practice and past JEE questions, so what you watch turns into marks.
- **Try before you pay.** Every chapter has a free preview.

Coaching works well for many students, and some use both. MathFlex simply gives you a lighter, cheaper way to fix the chapters that are holding your score back. For a longer look at why working chapter by chapter helps, read [why chapter-wise practice matters for JEE](/blog/why-chapter-wise-practice-matters-for-jee).

## FAQs

### Do I need to pay to try MathFlex?

No. Every chapter has a free 3–4 minute preview that you can watch without paying or signing up.

### Is MathFlex for JEE or for board exams?

Both. The chapters follow the Class 11 and 12 syllabus, and each chapter page shows how much of JEE Main it carries, so you can prioritise.

### How long do I get access?

A single chapter gives you 3 months. Class 11 Complete or Class 12 Complete gives you 1 year. The Class 11 + 12 combo gives you 2 years. Access is counted from the day you buy.

### Can I ask Karan a doubt directly?

Yes, through the 45-minute 1:1 call. You can book it from the [book a call page](/book-a-call).

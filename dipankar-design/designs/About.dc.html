Our story

Great JEE teaching shouldn't cost ₹2 lakh.

Karan Mittal cracked JEE and studied at IIT Delhi. Over the years he has guided more than 1,000 JEE aspirants, and he kept hearing the same thing: students were stuck on a few chapters, but the only way to get help was a coaching course that cost lakhs or an online bundle with the whole syllabus.

So we built Mathflex around one idea. Pay for the chapter you're stuck on, not the whole course. Every chapter is split into short parts, with practice after each one, so you can fix a weak topic in a weekend.

Part 1 of most chapters is free. Watch it, and if it clicks, the rest of the chapter starts at ₹149.
